Deployments Are Hard

Miki S-Y | October 12, 2026 | 2 min read

Deployments are hard. Why? Because robots aren't reliable enough. Why? Because models aren't good enough. Why? Because there isn't enough data. Why? Because there aren't enough deployments (and we've only scratched the surface on all the data that we need).

For deployments to work, the solution is a hybrid deployment approach: start with teleoperation until robots can operate autonomously, then transition to human-in-the-loop interventions and DAgger to collect more data, specifically in failure & recovery scenarios, improving reliability and teaching robots how to recover from failures as deployments scale.

There's an entire layer of infrastructure that needs to be built. That includes insurance, hardware maintenance, network systems, etc.
